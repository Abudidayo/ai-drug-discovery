## Decided
The reported Keynote jump was reproducible through actual pointer clicks: `web/prototypes/chapter-motion-check.mjs` sampled 109 frames after selecting Years of testing, with zero intermediate camera positions. `useNarrative` in `web/src/prototypes/round-three/narrative.ts` had explicitly selected instant mode and paused playback. Pointer selections now use a 900ms ease-out and resume playback; keyboard and reduced-motion selections remain immediate. Camera cleanup commits the visible transform before cancelling so rapid retargeting does not start from the previous destination.

The full-screen camera now transforms an HTML layer in `LaboratoryScene.tsx`, inside a separately scaled 1672×941 illustration plane. This avoids recalculating SVG geometry during camera motion. Internal CSS loops briefly hold while this camera moves, and resume at arrival. Tests assert that they resume and that pause/replay/reduced motion still work.

## Tried and ruled out
Changing only duration or adding will-change would not fix the explicit instant-selection branch. A controlled Chrome trace (1440×900 at 2× pixels, 4× CPU throttle, 2.5-second sample) recorded about 413ms Layout and 238ms Paint before the renderer change. The HTML-camera/cached-frame sample recorded about 5ms Layout and 57ms Paint. These are local work measurements, not a promised device frame rate; requestAnimationFrame sampling alone did not expose paint cost.

## Watch out
Do not put the camera transform back on the SVG g element. Preserve the separate resize projection and illustration coordinates when changing viewport layouts. Pointer chapter clicks must traverse intermediate positions, including the distinct compound focus; keyboard/reduced motion should not tween. Performance traces and measurements live in `.context/chapter-motion/`, generated by `web/prototypes/lab-performance.mjs`.
