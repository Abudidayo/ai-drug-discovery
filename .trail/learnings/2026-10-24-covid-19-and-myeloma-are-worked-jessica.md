## Decided
- The app serves any disease. COVID-19 (MONDO_0100096) and multiple myeloma (MONDO_0009693) are worked examples only.
- The headline number for judges is the multi-disease backtest in plan.md, step A12. It uses 30–50 held-out approved drug–disease pairs across at least five therapeutic areas. Each true drug is hidden among 19 popularity-matched decoys, with direct evidence masked. COVID-19/baricitinib is one row in it.
