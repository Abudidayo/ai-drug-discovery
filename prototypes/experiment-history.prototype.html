Compare drugs, keep your history

Question: can a learner compare candidate drugs within one experiment, request docking, return later and recover archived work - without creating another experiment just to compare drugs?

Fictional candidates and simulated calculations. No real scientific predictions, accounts or GPU calls. History is kept in memory for this demonstration and resets when the page reloads. This checks behavior, not the final visual design.

Proposed storage behavior

Each experiment saves its evidence, candidates and docking results in a shared database using an experiment identifier. Candidate selection does not create a new experiment. Database forks and experiment branching are excluded from the MVP.

The archive-during-docking walkthrough is an earlier exploratory behavior, not an MVP requirement. The first version may disable archiving while docking is active. Completed experiments can be archived and restored with their results retained.
