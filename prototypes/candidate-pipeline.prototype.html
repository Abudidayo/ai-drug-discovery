Prototype: how our app picks drug candidates

The question: does this flow hold up? You pick a disease, the app finds the proteins involved, pulls drugs that act on them, runs safety checks, ranks them, and you shortlist a few. Click through the scenarios to see the awkward cases: dead ends, drugs that hit several targets, injected drugs, and AI-generated molecules nobody has ever made.

Sample data, hand-picked for the demo. Drug names, targets and molecular properties are real (properties calculated with RDKit). Evidence scores and potencies are made-up stand-ins for what Open Targets and ChEMBL would return.
